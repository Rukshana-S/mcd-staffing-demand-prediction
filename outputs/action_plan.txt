
McDonald's Staffing Action Plan

Lunch Hours (12 PM–2 PM)
- Increase staffing by 1–2 employees during peak demand.

Dinner Hours (6 PM–8 PM)
- Schedule additional staff to reduce customer queues.

Low-Demand Hours (3 PM–5 PM)
- Reduce excess staffing where workload is low.

Weekend Planning
- Allocate more employees compared to weekdays.

Holiday Planning
- Prepare additional staff before expected demand spikes.

Outlet Monitoring
- High-volume outlets should receive flexible staffing support.
